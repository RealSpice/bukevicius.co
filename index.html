Why I'm writing

- - - - - - - - - -

After a nervous system regulation course, I realised how much of my life I had spent in fear and coping mechanisms. I couldn't enjoy what I already had, and I kept putting pressure on myself that I didn't need.

I'm starting to feel great with my new skills, but I feel like I needed this much earlier in my life. So I'm sharing my thoughts, so that people like me know a solution exists.

Before this, I spent years doing conversion and product work at companies like NordVPN and Showmax.

"I needed this much earlier in my life."

What I practice

  • Polyvagal theory

I learned much of this through Sarah Baldwin's You Make Sense course.

I'm a practitioner, not a therapist. What I share is my own experience, not medical or psychological advice.

Tools I built

- - - - - - - - - -

Parts Map

Open Parts Map →

When I finished Sarah Baldwin's You Make Sense course, I had no place to write my parts down, and no clear questions to ask myself after a hard moment. Parts Map asks the questions one at a time, the way the course did, and keeps the whole map of my parts: disconfirming evidence, photos, and a letter to the younger me.

The Mirror

Open The Mirror →

Agency over your state requires understanding where you are now. I struggled with a habit of following my states, so I built a simple tool to help me.
